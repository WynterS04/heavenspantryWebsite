Harvest of Hope Drive

Saturday November 21, 2026

Non-Denominational Church of Deliverance Fellowship Hall

Harvest of Hope is our annual fall outreach dedicated to ensuring families can gather around a full table during the Thanksgiving season. Through generous food donations and community support, we provide holiday meal boxes filled with nutritious essentials to families in need. Join us in sharing gratitude by giving, volunteering, or helping us distribute hope this season.

Season of Giving Drive

Saturday December 19, 2026

Non-Denominational Church of Deliverance Fellowship Hall

Season of Giving Drive is our winter outreach focused on bringing nourishment and joy to families during the Christmas season. In addition to our holiday food drive, we host a toy giveaway in conjunction with our outreach efforts, allowing community members to donate, drop off, or pick up toys for children in need. Together, we provide both meals and meaningful moments that help make the holidays brighter for local families.

Toys for Tots Holiday Drive

Saturday December 19, 2026

Non-Denominational Church of Deliverance Fellowship Hall

Our Toys for Tots Holiday Drive is dedicated to bringing joy to children in need during the Christmas season. Through generous donations from our community, we collect new toys and distribute them to local families to help make the holidays brighter. By giving a toy, you’re not just donating a gift — you’re sharing hope, love, and the spirit of the season.

Stay connected and keep up with our latest events by following us on social media.
