Join us in making a meaningful difference in our community. Volunteers play a vital role in boxing food packages, distributing meals during outreach events, promoting upcoming initiatives, assisting with event setup and cleanup, and so much more.

Sow a Seed of Hope

Your generosity helps us provide nutritious food and essential resources to families facing food insecurity in our community. Every contribution, no matter the size, allows Heaven’s Pantry to continue serving with compassion, dignity, and care.

Monetary

  • $10 helps provide fresh produce for a family
  • $25 helps stock pantry essentials for one household
  • $50 helps fill 2–3 food boxes for families in need
  • $100 helps support a community food distribution event
